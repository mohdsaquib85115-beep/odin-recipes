Kabab Recipe


Description

  • 1 lb minced chicken or mutton
  • 1 cup finely chopped onion
  • 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 green chilies, chopped
  • 1/2 cup fresh coriander, chopped
  • 1 tsp cumin powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 cup roasted gram flour or breadcrumbs
    steps
  1. Mix minced chicken or mutton with finely chopped onion, ginger-garlic paste, green chilies, coriander, cumin powder, garam masala, red chili powder, salt, and a little lemon juice.
  2. Add roasted gram flour or breadcrumbs to help bind the mixture.
  3. Knead everything well and let it marinate for 30–60 minutes.
  4. Shape the mixture into small flat or cylindrical kababs.
  5. Cook them on a hot grill, pan, or tawa with a little oil until they are golden brown and completely cooked.
  6. Serve hot with mint chutney, sliced onions, and lemon wedges.
