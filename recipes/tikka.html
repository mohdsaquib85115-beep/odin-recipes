Tikka Recipe


Description

    Ingredients
  • 1 lb boneless chicken, cut into bite-sized pieces
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp cumin powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp oil
    steps
  1. Cut boneless chicken into bite-sized pieces.
  2. Marinate the chicken with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, lemon juice, red chili powder, turmeric, cumin powder, garam masala, salt, and a little oil for at least 1–2 hours.
  3. Thread the pieces onto skewers along with chunks of onion and bell pepper.
  4. Grill, bake, or cook the skewers on a hot tawa until the chicken is tender, charred, and fully cooked.
  5. Brush with a little butter or ghee and serve hot with mint chutney, sliced onions, and lemon wedges.

To make **chicken tikka**, cut boneless chicken into bite-sized pieces and marinate them with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, lemon juice, red chili powder, turmeric, cumin powder, garam masala, salt, and a little oil. Let the chicken marinate for at least 1–2 hours so the flavors penetrate the meat. Thread the pieces onto skewers along with chunks of onion and bell pepper, then grill, bake, or cook them on a hot tawa until the chicken is tender, charred, and fully cooked. Brush with a little butter or ghee and serve hot with mint chutney, sliced onions, and lemon wedges.
