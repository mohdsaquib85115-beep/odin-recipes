Biryani Recipe


Description

    Ingredients
  • 1 lb chicken, cut into pieces
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 4 cups water
  • 2 tbsp oil or ghee
  • 1 large onion, thinly sliced
  • Whole spices (cinnamon, cardamom, cloves, bay leaves)
  • Saffron strands soaked in warm milk (optional)
  • Fresh coriander and mint leaves for garnish
    1. First marinate chicken with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, red chili powder, turmeric, garam masala, salt, and lemon juice for at least 30 minutes.
    2. Heat oil or ghee in a large pot and fry sliced onions until golden brown.
    3. Add whole spices such as cinnamon, cardamom, cloves, and bay leaves.
    4. Add the marinated chicken and cook until it is partially done.
    5. Meanwhile, boil basmati rice with salt and whole spices until it is about 70% cooked.
    6. Layer the partially cooked rice over the chicken, add fried onions, coriander, mint, and a little saffron-infused milk or food color.
    7. Cover the pot tightly and cook on low heat for about 20–25 minutes.
    8. Gently mix the layers and serve the hot biryani with raita or salad.
