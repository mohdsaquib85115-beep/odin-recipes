Nahari Recipe


Description

  • 1 lb beef or mutton, cut into bite-sized pieces
  • 2 tbsp oil or ghee
  • 1 cup sliced onions
  • 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1 tsp cumin powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 2 cups water or stock
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • 1 tbsp butter or ghee
  • Fresh coriander, sliced ginger, green chilies, fried onions, and lemon wedges for garnish
    steps
  1. Heat oil or ghee in a heavy pot and fry sliced onions until golden.
  2. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook briefly.
  3. Add beef or mutton along with salt, red chili powder, turmeric, coriander powder, cumin, and a traditional nihari spice mix.
  4. Cook until the meat is well browned.
  5. Add enough water or stock to cover the meat, bring it to a boil, then cover and simmer on very low heat for several hours until the meat becomes extremely tender and the broth turns rich and thick.
  6. Mix flour with water to make a smooth slurry and gradually add it to the pot while stirring to thicken the gravy.
  7. Simmer for another 15–20 minutes, then garnish with fresh coriander, sliced ginger, green chilies, fried onions, and a squeeze of lemon, and serve hot with naan.
